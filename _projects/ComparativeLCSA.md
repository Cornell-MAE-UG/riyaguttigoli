## Project Overview

<img src="{{ '/assets/images/5299/coverpic.png' | relative_url }}"
class="overview-image">

As part of **BEE 5299: Sustainable Development**, I completed a semester-long research project evaluating the sustainability of two leading grid-scale energy storage technologies: **lithium-ion Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS)** and **Pumped Hydro Energy Storage (PHES)**. Using a life cycle sustainability assessment (LCSA) framework, I compared the technologies across environmental, economic, and social dimensions to understand the tradeoffs that influence large-scale renewable energy deployment and long-term infrastructure planning.

---


---

## Project Highlights

- Conducted a cradle-to-grave **Life Cycle Sustainability Assessment (LCSA)** using a functional unit of **1 MWh of electricity delivered to the grid**.
- Performed **Environmental Life Cycle Assessment (E-LCA)**, **Life Cycle Costing (LCC)**, and **Social Life Cycle Assessment (S-LCA)** to compare the technologies from multiple systems-level perspectives.
- Developed life cycle inventory diagrams, comparative cost analyses, and impact assessments using data synthesized from academic literature, government publications, and industry reports.
- Evaluated engineering tradeoffs between environmental impacts, long-term economics, deployment flexibility, infrastructure requirements, and supply chain considerations.
- Produced a comprehensive technical report integrating engineering analysis, sustainability assessment methodologies, and evidence-based recommendations for grid-scale energy storage.
